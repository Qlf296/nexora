Hourly rate history (minimum wage changes)

When the Dutch minimum wage (WML) changes (every 1 Jan & 1 Jul), add a new line. The app applies the right rate per date automatically. Current WML since 01-07-2026: € 14.99.

Surcharges (contract art. 2.2)

Only the highest surcharge applies when they overlap (your contract). Overtime hours are paid on top of the fixed monthly base ("Overwerk 100%" line).

Sickness (contract art. 3 + Dutch law)

Minimum-wage floor (first 52 weeks)
Art. 7:629 BW: sick pay is 70%, but may never be below the statutory minimum wage during the first year of illness. Your wage IS the minimum wage → only the 2 waiting days can legally be deducted.

Relapse within 4 weeks counts as the same illness period → no new waiting days (applied automatically).

Tax estimate (loonheffing)

Calibrated on your 2026 payslips: tax ≈ base + 42.01% of gross above the reference. Matches your real payslips within €0.05. Adjust in January when tax tables change.

Dutch public holidays — paid at +%

Auto-calculated every year (Easter etc.). Toggle which ones your employer treats as official holidays.
